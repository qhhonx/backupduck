BackupDuck 0.4.1 lets you look inside an album before choosing it for backup.

Go to Backup > More settings > Specified albums. Tap a cover: it is chosen or deselected, as before. Long-press a cover and choose View Photos: the album opens in the library grid. Scroll, pinch and open photos; the circle at the top right chooses or deselects this album. Go back: the list should be at the same place, with your search and choices unchanged. Viewing an album never changes what is backed up. Try light and dark appearance and a large album.

Removing the last specified album leaves nothing in scope; turn All albums on to back up the whole library again.

Questions and feedback: Settings > Feedback group opens the Telegram tester group. Include the app version, device model, steps and error message. Do not include private photos or pairing codes.
