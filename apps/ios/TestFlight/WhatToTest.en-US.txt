BackupDuck 0.6.2 requires an iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Please try:
- Pair with the Pixel, choose what to back up, and start or pause earlier photos.
- Browse photos from the library, an album and backup history. Zoom, open a photo, and return to the same place.
- Open a video and a Live Photo. Check that the preview stays in place as playback starts.
