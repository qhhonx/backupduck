BackupDuck 0.5.0 is a redesign of the whole app. It needs iOS 18 or later. Update the Pixel receiver to 0.5.0 as well.

Please try:
- First run: pair with the Pixel, choose what to back up, then start or pause earlier photos on the Backup tab.
- Photos tab: open photos from the library, Backup History and the selection review; drag down to close; swipe through many photos; check Info.
- Select photos and back them up; the review sheet should show your selection.
- Lock the phone during a backup to see the Live Activity.
- Settings > Auto Backup: choose albums and media types.
- Light and dark appearance, and larger text.

Questions and feedback: Settings > Feedback group opens the Telegram tester group. Include the app version, device model, steps and error message. Do not include private photos or pairing codes.
