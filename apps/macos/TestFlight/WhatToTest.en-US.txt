BackupDuck 0.5.0 is a redesign of the whole app. It needs macOS 15 or later. Update the Pixel receiver to 0.5.0 as well.

Please try:
- First run: pair with the Pixel and choose what to back up; start or pause earlier photos in Backup overview.
- Photo Library, Folders and Backup History: zoom with the toolbar buttons and by pinching, scroll, select, and open photos in the viewer with Info.
- Add a folder and watch its photos and counts update.
- The menu bar menu shows backup status.
- Settings > Auto Backup: choose albums and media types.
- Light and dark appearance.

Questions and feedback: Settings > Feedback group opens the Telegram tester group. Include the app version, Mac model, steps and error message. Do not include private photos or pairing codes.
