BackupDuck 0.4.0 rebuilds the Mac photo library to match Photos.

Switch between Years, Months and All Photos; zoom All Photos with the toolbar and pinch through the grid and overview stops; select with click, Shift/Command, marquee and arrow keys; open photos and videos and page with keys, swipes and the trackpad. Check backup scope by media type and album, Settings > Cache (browsing thumbnails), folder backup, pairing with the current Android / Pixel receiver, pause/resume, and light/dark appearance.

This Mac build is sandboxed and uses TestFlight for updates. Access folders through the system picker; direct-download Mac data may live in a separate container. Google Photos cloud backup is independent of device receipt. Receiver download: https://backupduck.app/.

Questions and feedback: Settings > Feedback group opens the Telegram tester group. When reporting a problem, include the app version, device model, steps and error message. Do not include private photos or pairing codes.
