BackupDuck 0.4.1 lets you look inside an album before choosing it for backup.

Open Backup > More settings > Specified albums. Click a cover: it is chosen or deselected, as before. Secondary-click (Control-click) a cover and choose View Photos: the album opens in the sheet at its newest photos. Scroll and pinch to change the grid size; Choose Album at the top chooses or deselects it. Go back: the list should be at the same place, with your search and choices unchanged. Viewing an album never changes what is backed up. Also check that albums with only a few photos show them at once, and try light and dark appearance.

Removing the last specified album leaves nothing in scope; turn All albums on to back up the whole library again.

Questions and feedback: Settings > Feedback group opens the Telegram tester group. Include the app version, Mac model, steps and error message. Do not include private photos or pairing codes.
