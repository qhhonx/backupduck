BackupDuck Pro Subscription Terms

查看简体中文

Welcome week

Eligible iPhone and Mac App Store installations receive seven days of free access from the start of their welcome period. The welcome week ends without a charge or a subscription. You start a paid subscription only by choosing a plan and confirming the purchase with Apple.

Plans and billing

The monthly and annual plans include the same backup features and have no BackupDuck transfer quota. Apple charges your Apple Account for the full period when you confirm the purchase. An annual plan is billed for the full year at once. Its monthly equivalent and savings are shown for comparison with twelve monthly payments. Check the total and billing period in Apple’s purchase confirmation for your storefront.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. For the U.S. storefront, Apple may charge for renewal in the 24 hours before the next period begins. To avoid that charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. After cancellation, you can use Pro through the current paid period unless Apple revokes access. Apple handles plan changes and billing. To request a refund, use Report a Problem. Eligibility depends on Apple’s terms for your country or region and applicable law.

Restoring access and what happens when it ends

To recover an active subscription, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase and choose Restore Purchases on the Pro page. When access ends, unfinished backups pause and keep their progress. You can still browse and export your photos. With active access, you can continue unfinished backups.

备份鸭 Pro 订阅条款

七天免费体验

符合条件的 iPhone 和 Mac App Store 版，从欢迎体验开始起可免费使用七天。体验到期时不会扣费或自动订阅。只有在你选择方案并通过 Apple 确认购买后，才会开始付费订阅。

方案与扣费

月付和年付包含相同的备份功能，备份鸭不设传输额度。确认购买时，Apple 会通过你的 Apple 账户收取整期费用。年付一次收取全年费用，折算月价和折扣以连续支付十二个月的月付总价为比较基准。实际收费总额和计费周期以你所在商店地区的 Apple 购买确认页为准。

续费、取消与退款

订阅会自动续费。你可以在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。在中国大陆商店，Apple 可能会在下一计费周期开始前 24 小时内扣款。如需避免续费，请在本期结束前至少 24 小时取消。取消后可使用至本期结束，Apple 撤销权益的情况除外。方案变更和账单由 Apple 处理。退款可通过 Apple「报告问题」申请，是否符合退款条件取决于你所在国家或地区适用的 Apple 条款和法律。

恢复购买与权益到期

如需找回有效订阅，请使用购买时的 Apple 账户，并在 Pro 页选择「恢复购买」。权益到期后，未完成的备份会暂停并保留进度，你仍可浏览和导出照片。恢复有效权益后，可继续未完成的备份。
