Privacy / 隐私说明

Updated: October 10, 2026

Your photos and devices

BackupDuck reads photos and videos you allow and sends selected originals directly to your paired receiver over the local network. Pairing, settings, transfer history, temporary files and diagnostic records are kept on your devices. The app does not provide a hosted photo library or a BackupDuck account.

The Android receiver saves gallery copies locally. Google Photos, when separately enabled by you, performs cloud backup under Google's settings and policies. Receiver delivery is not confirmation of cloud upload.

Permissions and sharing

Photo access lets the sender read your chosen library. Camera access scans pairing codes; local network access connects your devices. Mac folder access applies to folders you select. Optional Android accessibility-based space management reads Google Photos backup indications and performs the cleanup actions you enable.

Diagnostics and saved-photo records can be exported by you. They may contain filenames, device information and operational details; review them before sharing. Exporting a file does not itself upload it to us.

Website and updates

The website and update metadata are served through GitHub Pages or Vercel; app downloads are served through GitHub. Legacy update addresses remain on Vercel during the hosting transition. Requests to these services expose normal network information such as IP address and request headers to the providers. Language preference is saved locally in the browser.

Control and contact

You can revoke permissions in system settings and control backup, retained files and diagnostics in the app. Keep source originals until you have independently verified your backups. For support, use GitHub Issues; do not post private photos, pairing credentials or unredacted logs publicly.

中文说明

备份鸭读取你允许访问的照片和视频，经局域网发送至配对的接收设备。配对、设置、传输记录、临时文件及运行记录保存在设备上；本产品不提供自己的云端相册或账号。Pixel 上的 Google 相册由你单独开启并按其规则上传，设备接收成功不等于云端备份完成。

照片、相机、局域网及文件夹权限用于相应的备份和配对功能。可选的 Android 无障碍空间管理读取 Google 相册备份提示并执行你启用的清理操作。导出诊断或相册保存记录不会自动上传，但文件可能含文件名和设备信息，请检查后再分享。

官网和更新信息由 GitHub Pages 或 Vercel 提供，应用下载由 GitHub 提供；迁移期间旧版更新地址继续由 Vercel 提供。请求会向服务商提供 IP 地址等常规网络信息。你可以在系统设置中撤销权限，并在应用中控制备份和本地保存。公开反馈请避免上传私人照片、配对凭据或未脱敏日志。
