# Product brief

## In one sentence

BackupDuck sends the photos on your iPhone and Mac, at full quality, straight to
your own Pixel and saves them in its Photos app. Google Photos on that Pixel
backs them up as usual. Photos never pass through BackupDuck's servers.

## Why people want it

An older Pixel is the cheapest way to back up a large library:

- **Pixel (first generation): unlimited Original-quality backup** in Google Photos.
- **Pixel 2 to Pixel 5: unlimited Storage-saver backup.**

Say this prominently, on the store page, in the website and in first run. Put
the conditions in small print next to it: the offer is Google's, applies to
photos backed up from that Pixel, and Google may change it. Before shipping any
such sentence, check it against Google's current Pixel offer page (constitution
§ Copy). Being careful in the small print does not mean hiding the main reason
people choose the product.

## Who it is for

People who take and keep photos on an iPhone or a Mac, have a Pixel at hand
(often an old one), and want a dependable full-quality copy without learning
how it works.

## What they need to do

1. **First time:** connect the two devices and start the backup.
2. **After that:** forget about it. New photos go over by themselves; earlier
   photos keep backing up in the background once started.
3. **When they wonder:** see at a glance that their photos are safe.
4. **When something goes wrong:** understand what happened and what to do.
5. **When the Pixel fills up:** free space without risk.

## What we promise

- Full quality, sent directly; never through our cloud.
- Nothing lost, nothing duplicated; interrupted work resumes by itself.
- Right by default; no tuning needed.
- The truth about state: received, saved to the Pixel's Photos, and backed up
  by Google Photos are three separate facts. Each is said separately, and
  unknown stays unknown.

## Words people never need

Original (as a file concept), receiver and sender, queue, concurrency, cache,
verification, receipt, relay, LAN, certificate, token. The interface says
photos, your Pixel, Photos and Wi‑Fi (`copy-glossary.zh.md`).

## Success

- A new user is paired and backing up within five minutes.
- Day to day, nothing needs doing.
- Settings holds at most eight rows. Everything else is automatic or in
  Advanced.

## Not in this version

Accounts and cloud storage, photos hosted by us, a rule editor for backups.

## Paid features

There is no membership surface yet. Everyone gets every capability, Advanced
included. Build anything that may later become paid behind an entitlement check
that is always granted today and that a hidden test switch can turn off, so its
effect can be tried before anything is shown to users. Advanced means "for
fewer people", not "paid".
