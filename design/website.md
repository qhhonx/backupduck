# Website

The official site (`site/`) is the first public face of the brand. It shares the apps' promise, words and visual language, with their current
screens as its imagery. It is a page that answers, in order, what
a visitor asks: what is this, is it true for me, how do I start, is it safe.

## Principles

- **One promise first.** The headline is the product's promise from
  `product-brief.md`; conditions sit right under it in small print, never hidden.
- **Show the real apps.** Imagery is current catalog captures of the
  iPhone, Mac and Pixel screens (synthetic data).
- **Same language as the apps.** Words follow `copy-glossary.zh.md`; the three
  step marks (yellow circle, blue wing, orange sparkle, no numerals) carry the
  three steps exactly as first run does; the duck appears for brand moments only.
- **Visual language V1–V7.** System font stack, the role tokens, white or black
  backgrounds (no tinted canvas), the shared radii; light and dark.
- **Quiet and fast.** One column of sections on a phone, no autoplaying video
  with sound, works without JavaScript except the download chooser.
- **Keep the contracts.** Download links and their fallbacks, `site/api/`
  (update feeds read by installed apps), the privacy page URL and existing
  anchors keep working.

## Sections

1. **Header**: the duck mark and name; Download; language switch (中文 / EN).
2. **Promise.** "Bigger than iCloud. Simpler than a NAS. And free." Below: "One
   old Pixel backs up all your photos to Google Photos, full quality, without
   limits." Primary action: Download (the visitor's platform). Small print:
   "Original Pixel: unlimited Original quality. Pixel 2 to 5: unlimited Storage
   saver. The offer is Google's." The Chinese copy is the first-run welcome copy
   (`flows.md#1-first-run`). Image: the iPhone hub beside the Pixel receive page.
3. **Three steps**, with the three marks: Install BackupDuck on a Pixel ·
   Connect the two devices · Photos back up to Google Photos. One line each, as
   in first run.
4. **Why an old Pixel.** The Google Photos offer for original Pixels, in plain
   words and a small table (Pixel / Pixel XL: Original quality, unlimited;
   Pixel 2–5: Storage saver, unlimited), and that the Pixel frees its own space.
5. **What it does for you**, four short items with a capture each: backs up on
   its own over Wi‑Fi; keeps originals, Live Photos and videos; Mac folders,
   drives and SD cards; frees space on the Pixel automatically.
6. **Private by design.** Photos travel only between your own devices;
   BackupDuck has no cloud of its own. Link to the privacy page.
7. **Download**: iPhone / iPad, Mac, Pixel (Android receiver), each with its
   requirement line and install route as today; the community link.
8. **Questions**: a few short answers in the apps' words (iCloud needed?,
   does "received" mean in Google Photos?, what if the Pixel fills up?, overnight
   backup on iPhone, folders and SD cards, keep another copy).
9. **Footer**: privacy, source code, feedback group, version.
